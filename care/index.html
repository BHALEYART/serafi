Sizing and care

How to find your ring size, how to size a stack, and how to keep everything looking new.

Find your ring size

Measure a ring you own

  1. Pick a ring that fits the finger you’re shopping for.
  2. Measure the inside diameter, edge to edge, in millimeters.
  3. Find the closest diameter below. If you’re between sizes, choose the larger one.

Measurements are approximate. Fingers change size with temperature and time of day, so measure in the afternoon when your hands are warm.

Sizing a stack

  • Bands wider than about 3mm, and stacks of four or more rings, often feel snug. Many people go up a half size for the stack.
  • Put your widest band at the base of the finger and finer rings above it.
  • Eternity rings can’t be resized, so confirm your size before ordering one.

Jewelry care

  • Take rings off for the gym, swimming, gardening and cleaning.
  • Put jewelry on after lotion, perfume and hairspray.
  • Clean with warm water, a drop of mild dish soap and a soft toothbrush. Rinse and pat dry.
  • Store pieces separately so diamonds don’t scratch softer gold.
  • Have prongs on stone settings checked about once a year.
